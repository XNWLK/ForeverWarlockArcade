# Changelog

## v0.2.5
- The Forever Warlock Sim's newest numbers: Destro Incinerate Imp is retired (five builds now), Wrack Succubus casts Shadow Bolt when its DoTs have under 4 s left, the other builds use a smarter Life Tap, and Troll Berserking lasts 15 s (patch notes). Build DPS, spell damage and stat weights in Higher or Lower and Guess the DPS follow.

## v0.2.4
- More racials from the WoW: Forever patch notes, through the Forever Warlock Sim: Orc Blood Fury has a 2 minute cooldown (was 3), Undead Touch of the Grave a 15 s internal cooldown (was 25). Build DPS per race in Higher or Lower and Guess the DPS follows.

## v0.2.3
- The Forever Warlock Sim's default fight is 3 minutes again (fights will be longer than 2 minutes): build DPS, spell damage and stat weights in Higher or Lower and Guess the DPS follow, about 1-2% lower.

## v0.2.2
- The racials from the WoW: Forever patch notes, through the Forever Warlock Sim: Human Ruthless is +1% crit and The Human Spirit +10% Spirit; Orc loses Savage and gets Command back (+5% demon damage). Build DPS per race, spell damage and stat weights in Higher or Lower and Guess the DPS follow.

## v0.2.1
- The newest numbers from the Forever Warlock Sim: the Aff Pact Shadow Bolt build is retired (six builds now), "SM Ruin (classic)" is just **SM Ruin**, and build DPS and stat weights follow the sim's latest demon melee numbers. Higher or Lower and Guess the DPS use them.

## v0.2.0
- Ten more games - **26** in all:
  - **Guess the DPS** - guess the sim's own numbers (build DPS, spell damage, casts, stat worth); a daily round.
  - **Crack the Opener** - a hidden opener of four casts, gold and silver marks, eight tries; a daily one.
  - **Spell Lock!** - stop enemy casts with your Felhunter; casts that get through hit for their real damage.
  - **Ritual of Summoning** - repeat the demon runes, one more every round.
  - **Grimoire Pairs** - find the pairs of spell icons in three sizes.
  - **Drain Snake** - steer your Drain Soul beam to the souls.
  - **Fel Runner** - jump the fire and the Core Hounds, duck the bats.
  - **Shard Stack** - fill whole rows with falling shards; rows cast spells for their real damage.
  - **Pentagram Push** - push the soul shards onto the pentagrams, eight hand-made rituals.
  - **Demon Gate** - spell towers with the spells' real numbers against fifteen waves.
- The game picker shows sixteen games a page: the arrows, the mouse wheel or Left / Right turn the page.
- Installs into the live client's folder: `World of Warcraft\_forever_\Interface\AddOns`.

## v0.1.0
- The arcade: a game picker, in the browser and as an addon (`/arcade` in the game).
- **Shard & Mult**, a poker-hand roguelike with Warlock spells: Shadow Bolt, Corruption, Immolate and Incinerate are
  the suits, J/Q/K are demons, talents are your jokers, Molten Core bosses are the blinds and Ragnaros waits at ante 8.
  Cards crit with the real spell crit of the Forever Warlock Sim's standard character. Your run is saved.
- **DoT Juggler**: keep Corruption, Bane of Agony and Immolate ticking on up to six training dummies for 90 seconds.
  Real cast times, global cooldown, spell queue, mana, damage per tick and Life Tap from the sim. Your best DPS is kept.
- **Higher or Lower**, **Warlockle**, **Warlock Ware**, **Shadow Pegs**, **Tap Tap DPS** and **Demon 2048**: all eight games are playable.
- Fixed: after winning a blind in Shard & Mult, the Collect button could not be clicked. Enter collects too.
- **Soul Circles**: the Tap Tap DPS rotation with aim - click the spells as their rings close in, hold casts and follow the ball. Two keys (Z / X, changeable) work too.
- Tap Tap DPS: pick your own keys with **Change keys** (lanes and Life Tap). They are saved.
- Seven more games: **Warlock Connections** and **Iconle** (new puzzles every day), **Type to Cast**, **Spell Gems**,
  **Felsweeper**, **Flappy Imp** and **Perfect Rotation**. Sixteen games in all.
- A new look: rounded, softly lit panels and buttons, shadows, glows when you point at things, panels that fade in,
  embers drifting through the window, and a game picker with filters (Daily, Rhythm, Action, Puzzle, Cards & Quiz).
  New sounds on the web page. Fixed: on the web page, long texts with extra line spacing were cut off at the bottom.
